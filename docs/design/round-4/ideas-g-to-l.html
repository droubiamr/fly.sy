Six ideas in fly.sy’s own colours

Same logo, same passport green, same calm grey, same Readex Pro. What changes from idea to idea is the shape of things: the buttons, the background, and how the answer is drawn.

Every phone shows the same page: Türkiye to Damascus, Syrian passport, in Arabic. Under each one is its set of buttons. Switch your device to dark mode to see the dark versions. Ideas can be mixed: one idea’s buttons with another’s answer works.
