fly.sy · Redesign, round 2 · 1 October 2026

Three looks for the new fly.sy

You asked for a new visual identity on top of the new structure. Here is the same route page, Türkiye to Damascus, in three directions. The layout is the one from the plan; only the look changes.

They are in Arabic because most visitors see Arabic first, and a look that fails in Arabic fails the site. All three come in light and dark; switch your device’s theme to see the other. Already decided: sources one tap away, the new tabs, and site-wide search. The structure, the search-engine safeguards and the order of work are in the full plan.

What stays the same

  • Status colours and iconsGreen dot for open; amber, red and grey labels with an icon for the rest. Colour is never the only signal.
  • Arabic firstRight to left by default, English mirrored. Fonts are self-hosted and split by script, so an Arabic page never loads Latin glyphs it won’t use.
  • ReadableBody text 16px or larger, 44px touch targets, contrast checked in light and dark.
  • The markThe Syria outline stays as the logo and takes the new colours. The map is redrawn in each palette.
