  • Map first. The route drawn on the real map, edge to edge, before anything else.
  • The question is a sign. From and to on one green panel; the passport as three plates under it.
  • One sign per way. Plane, car, or both, then the destination, how you get there and the total time. The airlines that fly it appear as white tiles, like services on a motorway sign.
  • Restrictions are plates. “Syrians only” hangs under its sign, the way a lorry or height limit would.
  • Every flight below. On a plain board, with only the unconfirmed ones marked.
